Install in a few minutes.

Android asks for a few permissions because it does not let apps replace the lock screen or control center outright. Liquid Glass sits on top of them instead.

Download and open the APKAllow "install from this source" for your browser when Android asks.
Press Home and choose Liquid GlassPick "Always" so it becomes your home screen.
Open Glass Settings and tick the set-up listDisplay over other apps, Accessibility (control center), Notification access and Change system settings.
Switch on the glass lock screenThen tap "Edit lock screen" to pick your font, colour and clock position.
Good to know. Liquid Glass is a launcher plus overlays, not a new operating system, so it cannot remove your phone's own lock screen or control center. It covers them. Your PIN or fingerprint still protects the phone, and swiping up on the glass lock screen brings up the normal unlock. Notification and settings access stays on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
